## Experiment data and hardware references

- The early webcam experiment used AMD's quantized YOLOv8 model and COCO labels from the [Ryzen AI Software YOLOv8 tutorial](https://github.com/amd/RyzenAI-SW/tree/bb02ad48b6ed5c304ffd1ddb005d2fed5d4a6046/tutorial/yolov8). See the [webcam report](experiments/002_webcam_yolov8.md).
- The model compatibility sweep used MobileNetV2, ResNet50, nano-YOLOX, RetinaFace, and PointPainting FPN from AMD's [multi-model demo](https://github.com/amd/RyzenAI-SW/tree/bb02ad48b6ed5c304ffd1ddb005d2fed5d4a6046/demo/multi_model) and its [resource archive](https://www.xilinx.com/bin/public/openDownload?filename=resource_multi_model_demo.zip). The [compatibility report](experiments/003_model_compatibility.md) records the separate ResNet50 test from AMD's ONNX benchmark checkout.
- The speech experiments measured recognition on 210 utterances from the LibriSpeech `test-clean` split by Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, and Sanjeev Khudanpur. [OpenSLR LibriSpeech](https://www.openslr.org/12) · [Speech experiment](experiments/011_parakeet_npu.md) · [Full NPU encoder experiment](experiments/012_all_npu_encoder.md)
- The ONNX experiments used [AMD Ryzen AI Software 1.7](https://ryzenai.docs.amd.com/en/1.7/), the [Vitis AI execution provider](https://ryzenai.docs.amd.com/en/1.7/modelrun.html), [AMD Quark for ONNX](https://quark.docs.amd.com/latest/onnx/basic_usage_onnx.html), and [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime). See the [operator placement report](experiments/004_why_models_run.md).
- Hardware references include AMD's [XDNA architecture overview](https://www.amd.com/en/technologies/xdna.html), [AIE-ML array features](https://docs.amd.com/r/en-US/am020-versal-aie-ml/AIE-ML-Array-Features), [array-interface DMA specification](https://docs.amd.com/r/en-US/am020-versal-aie-ml/Array-Interface-DMA-Memory-Mapped-AXI4-Master-Interface), and [XRT-SMI guide](https://ryzenai.docs.amd.com/en/main/xrt_smi.html).

## App and development dependencies

- **Windows app and LAN interface.** [Microsoft .NET](https://github.com/dotnet/runtime), [ASP.NET Core](https://github.com/dotnet/aspnetcore), [Windows App SDK](https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK), [WinUI](https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml), and [ZXing.Net](https://github.com/micjahn/ZXing.Net) for pairing QR codes. The [project file](apps/npu_chat_winui/NpuChat.csproj) pins the NuGet dependencies.
- **Local inference and experiments.** [CPython](https://github.com/python/cpython), [NumPy](https://github.com/numpy/numpy), [ml_dtypes](https://github.com/jax-ml/ml_dtypes), [ONNX](https://github.com/onnx/onnx), [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), [PyTorch](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch), [Hugging Face Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers), [Tokenizers](https://github.com/huggingface/tokenizers), [Safetensors](https://github.com/huggingface/safetensors), [Hugging Face Hub](https://github.com/huggingface/huggingface_hub), and [Jinja](https://github.com/pallets/jinja) for chat templates. The vision probes use [OpenCV](https://github.com/opencv/opencv).
- **Audio and attachments.** [python-sounddevice](https://github.com/spatialaudio/python-sounddevice) and [PortAudio](https://github.com/PortAudio/portaudio) for recording, [SoundFile](https://github.com/bastibe/python-soundfile) for experiment audio, [Phonemizer](https://github.com/bootphon/phonemizer), [eSpeak NG](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng), and [espeakng-loader](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/espeakng-loader) for phonemes, [Spotify Pedalboard](https://github.com/spotify/pedalboard) for pitch, and [pypdf 6.21.4](https://pypi.org/project/pypdf/6.21.4/) for selectable PDF text.
- **Upstream NPU Dictate standalone build.** [Pillow](https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow), [pystray](https://github.com/moses-palmer/pystray), [uv](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv), and [PyInstaller](https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller). See the [upstream app's requirements](apps/npu_dictate/requirements.txt) and [build script](apps/npu_dictate/build/build.ps1).
- **Tool and skill formats.** [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25), [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/specification), and OpenCode's [skills](https://opencode.ai/docs/skills/), [MCP](https://opencode.ai/docs/mcp-servers/), and [plugin](https://opencode.ai/docs/plugins/) conventions inform the app's import support. Repository discovery and import use the [GitHub REST API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest).
- **Installed development and design references.** [OpenCode](https://opencode.ai/), [UI UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill), [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), and [Emil Kowalski's design engineering skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills).
